STRIDELY WEBSITE (HTML, CSS, JavaScript)

HOW TO OPEN
Double-click index.html. Keep all folders together.

PAGES
index.html     Home
products.html  Products (category and budget filters)
about.html     About Us
contact.html   Contact + demo order form + FAQ
landing.html   Landing page (open landing.html?v=A or ?v=B for the two variants)

HOW TO GET A LIVE URL (free)
Option 1: go to app.netlify.com/drop and drag the whole stridely-website folder in.
Option 2: GitHub Pages (upload the files, then Settings > Pages).

CUSTOMISE
- Replace the shoe drawings in /images (name.svg) with real photos: change the src in products.html.
- Colours are at the top of css/style.css.
- Forms are demo only. Nothing is sent or stored.
- Testimonials and contact details are fictional samples; keep them labelled.
