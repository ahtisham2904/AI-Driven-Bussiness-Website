About Us

A shoe store that started with a campus problem.

Our story

Stridely began as an idea between lectures: good shoes should not cost a month of pocket money, and they should still feel good after ten hours on your feet.

We choose a small range of comfortable, honest styles and sell them online, so students and young professionals can order from their phone and have shoes delivered to their door.

Our purpose

Help every customer find a good fit and good value, with clear prices and a simple exchange policy.

Our promise

  • Clear prices with no hidden fees
  • Free delivery over Rs. 5,000
  • 7-day exchange for unworn shoes
  • We only ask for the details needed to deliver your order
